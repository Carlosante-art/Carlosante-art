Four domains, one idea: model the individual from their own data, not the average. Numbers are computed in code, the AI only interprets, and the person has the last word.

#### In plain words

- **Selvra** gives AI a memory of you that you own. Today every new chat starts from zero. Selvra keeps what matters about you, shows where each piece came from, and only lets an AI add something after it has been checked.
- **Stillra** helps people with type 1 diabetes prepare for doctor visits. It reads their glucose sensor, works out the numbers doctors use, and writes a short summary, with safety checks so it never tells a patient something harmful.
- **Forsyne** connects sleep, training, food and stress to predict how you will feel tomorrow, then checks itself against how you actually felt.
- **Motiq** listens to a musician's own songs and shows their personal style: the patterns they keep returning to and how their music is changing over time.

What I do in all four: collect messy data from many sources, clean and structure it, calculate reliable numbers from it, and turn it into insights a person can understand and trust.
